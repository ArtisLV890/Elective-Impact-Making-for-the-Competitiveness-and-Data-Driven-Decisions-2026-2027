# Part A. Idea 2: required training for social-studies teachers

## A1. Legal basis: does the Cabinet have the power?

**The chain of authority**

- **Education Law, Art. 14, point 32:** the Cabinet "determines the procedure for teachers' professional development" (*nosaka pedagogu profesionālās kompetences pilnveides kārtību*).
- **Regulation No. 569 is issued under this delegation** (its preamble cites Art. 14, points 13 and 32, and Art. 48(1)).
- **Education Law, Art. 51(1), point 4:** improving professional competence is already a teacher's legal **duty**.
- **Education Law, Art. 49(3):** development happens through self-study and continuing-education courses.

**Does "procedure" include "required content"?**

- The delegation speaks of *procedure* (*kārtība*). But the Cabinet has consistently read it to include **what** teachers must learn, not only **how**.
  - **Point 18:** lists the topic areas a programme must cover.
  - **Point 20:** at least **6 hours of child-rights protection** for every teacher. This one is also backed by the Child Rights Protection Law, Art. 5.1.
  - **Point 20.1** (Regulation No. 354, 10.06.2025, in force 01.09.2025): PE teachers who teach swimming must do **24 hours** of swimming instruction and water safety, plus first aid and a 4-hour refresher every 5 years. **This rests only on the Education Law delegation.**
  - **Point 3:** teachers in special education must complete a specific 72-hour programme (21.3).
- **Conclusion:** a topic-specific requirement for teachers of a specific subject is **within the Cabinet's established practice** under Art. 14, point 32. No change to the Education Law is needed.

## A2. Constitutional and general-law checks

| Check | Assessment |
|---|---|
| **Within the delegation** (a Cabinet regulation may not exceed its law) | Yes. It regulates professional development, as the law delegates, and follows the swimming precedent |
| **Proportionality** | Strong: it applies only to teachers of the relevant subjects; it sits inside the existing 36 hours (no new hours); there is a 3-year transition; courses are free and online |
| **Equal treatment** (Constitution, Art. 91) | Treating subject groups differently is justified by the content they teach, as with PE teachers and swimming |
| **Teachers' professional freedom** (Education Law, Art. 52(1), point 5.1: freedom to choose methods and materials) | Not affected. The rule concerns teachers' own training, not how they teach |
| **Labour law** | Training within the existing 36-hour duty does not change working time. Teachers already have a right to 30 paid days every 3 years (Art. 52(1), point 4) |
| **EU law** | No EU rule restricts it. It supports the EU Financial Literacy Strategy (COM(2025) 681) |

## A3. The draft text

**Amendment 1: a new point 20.2** (with the exact subject names from Regs. 747 and 416):

> **20.2** Vispārējās izglītības pedagogs, kas māca mācību priekšmetu "Sociālās zinības" pamatizglītības 7.–9. klasē vai mācību kursu "Sociālās zinātnes I", "Sociālās zinātnes II" vai "Sociālās zinības un vēsture" vispārējās vidējās izglītības pakāpē, triju gadu laikā pilnveido profesionālo kompetenci finanšu pratībā, tai skaitā finanšu drošībā digitālajā vidē, vismaz 12 stundu apjomā šo noteikumu 21. punktā norādītajās programmās.

*In English:* a general-education teacher who teaches "Social studies" in grades 7–9, or the courses "Social sciences I", "Social sciences II" or "Social studies and history" at secondary level, completes at least 12 hours of financial literacy training, including financial safety online, every 3 years, in programmes listed in point 21.

**Optional:** add schools' own specialised courses with economics content: *"… vai citu mācību kursu, kura saturā ir ekonomikas vai finanšu jautājumi"* ("or another course whose content covers economics or finance"). This is broader but less precise. Lawyers usually prefer the closed list.

**Amendment 2: add the topic to the list in point 18.1,** so programmes can be classified clearly:

> … finanšu pratība un finanšu drošība …

*In English:* "… financial literacy and financial safety …"

**Amendment 3: transitional point,** modelled on point 32, which did the same for the swimming rule:

> **33.** Šo noteikumu 20.2 punktā noteikto prasību piemēro ar 2028. gada 1. septembri, un pedagogs to izpilda līdz 2031. gada 31. augustam. Izglītības iestādes vadītājs šo noteikumu 20.2 punktā noteiktās prasības izpildē ieskaita finanšu pratības programmas, kas apgūtas šo noteikumu 21. punktā norādītajās programmās laikposmā no 2027. gada 1. septembra.

*In English:* the requirement applies from 1 September 2028, and teachers must comply by 31 August 2031. The school head counts financial literacy programmes completed in approved programmes since 1 September 2027 toward it.

This lets the modules from Idea 1 count from the start.

**Point 15 is not changed.** The 12 hours count toward the 36.

## A4. The procedure (Cabinet Rules of Procedure, Reg. No. 606)

| Step | Rule | What happens | Our entry point |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Drafting | Reg. 606; ex-ante impact rules | **IZM** drafts the amendment and the **annotation** (problem, solution, budget impact, administrative burden, consultation) | Send IZM the draft text and a two-page annotation-style case |
| 2. Public participation | Reg. 606, points 32 and 38; Cabinet Regulation No. 639 (15.10.2024) on public participation | The draft is published on the **TAP portal**; anyone can comment after logging in | Submit a formal opinion; ask partners to do the same. **[verify the minimum consultation period in Reg. 639]** |
| 3. Coordination | Reg. 606, points 51–52 | IZM sends the draft to ministries and institutions. These include: **Latvian Association of Local Governments** (point 52.2.3) where municipalities are affected (they own most schools and employ teachers); **the employers' confederation (LDDK) and the Free Trade Union Confederation (LBAS)** (point 52.2.7) where employers and employees are affected. The teachers' union LIZDA belongs to LBAS | Talk to LIZDA early; the "inside the 36 hours" design answers the workload concern |
| 4. Opinions and objections | Reg. 606, points 56–67 | Opinions within the set deadline (extendable by up to 3 working days); **silence counts as agreement** (point 65); objections lead to a revised draft or an inter-institutional meeting | Follow the TAP file; respond to objections with data |
| 5. Cabinet | Reg. 606, point 77 | NGOs may be invited to Cabinet committee or Cabinet meetings; the NGO Memorandum Council has a representative (point 105.16) | Ask to be heard if the draft is contested |
| 6. Publication | Reg. 606, point 32 | Published in *Latvijas Vēstnesis*; in force as stated | — |

**Who can start it:**

- **IZM**, on its own initiative or on an instruction from the Cabinet.
- **The Saeima cannot amend a Cabinet regulation.** But it (or its committee or subcommittee) can hear IZM, ask for a report, or adopt a decision calling on the Cabinet. The subcommittee is the right pressure point.
- **Latvijas Banka** can recommend it, as part of its legal task to "advise the Saeima and the Cabinet" (Law on Latvijas Banka, Art. 5(1), point 16) and to promote financial literacy (point 17).

## A5. What the annotation must show

1. **The problem, with data:**
   - teachers' insufficient knowledge of simple and compound interest (Latvijas Banka 2024);
   - 36% of 9th graders never learn about money at school;
   - 48% offered "easy money" online, 18% asked to be money mules;
   - fraud losses €8.5m (2023) → €23.7m (2025);
   - the OECD (2026) calls school financial literacy "essential".
2. **Why regulation:** voluntary training reaches too few teachers. Peru: only 43% completed the voluntary training, and a third never taught the lessons.
3. **Budget impact:** **none or minimal,** if courses are free (Latvijas Banka content delivered by VIAA within its existing teacher-support functions, Reg. 207, points 3.5 and 4.13). Teachers' time is within the existing 36-hour duty. **[verify]** who pays for teacher training in practice: the school founder, the state support system or the teacher.
4. **Administrative burden:** heads already plan and record the 36 hours (point 15). One more topic adds almost nothing.
5. **Consultation:** IZM, VIAA, Latvijas Banka, the Latvian Association of Local Governments, LBAS/LIZDA, LDDK, NGOs.

## A6. Enforcement and compliance

| Mechanism | Source | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| The school head plans each teacher's training and must include the required topics | Reg. 569, point 15 | Main mechanism |
| Certificates on the Annex 1 form, kept in the teacher's records | Reg. 569, point 23 | Evidence |
| Accreditation checks documents on teachers' education and professional development | Cabinet Regulation No. 325 (04.06.2024) on accreditation | Moderate. **[verify]** whether accreditation looks at individual required topics |
| Consequence of non-compliance | No specific sanction in Reg. 569. A breach of duty under Education Law Art. 51(1), point 4 can be addressed by the employer | Weak, as for all training rules |

**Our reading:** compliance will rest on **school heads** and on **courses being easy to take** (Idea 1). That is why Ideas 1 and 2 must go together.

## A7. Risks and fallbacks

| Risk | Likelihood | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| LIZDA objects on workload | Medium | Inside the existing 36 hours; free; online; 3-year transition |
| Municipalities object on cost | Low–medium | Courses delivered via VIAA at no charge; no new hours |
| "Why finance and not other topics?" | Medium | The same reasoning as the swimming rule: a documented knowledge gap plus a risk to pupils |
| Not enough courses by 2028 | Medium | Idea 1 must run first; the transitional point recognises courses from 2027 |
| Political will lacking | Medium | Saeima subcommittee; Latvijas Banka recommendation; OECD action plan |

**Fallbacks, from weaker to stronger, if the full rule fails:**

1. **Topic only:** add "financial literacy" to point 18.1. This signals priority but requires nothing.
2. **Municipal priority:** municipal education boards plan teachers' training (Education Law, Art. 17(2), point 10). Ask the larger municipalities to prioritise it.
3. **The Idea 1 course alone,** promoted by IZM to school heads.

# Part B. Idea 4: measure and show

## B1. Repeating Latvijas Banka's 9th-grade assessment every 2 years

**Legal basis**

- **Law on Latvijas Banka, Art. 5(1), point 17:** Latvijas Banka "promotes public financial literacy and the acquisition of economic knowledge". Measuring pupils' financial literacy serves this task directly.
- **Art. 5(1), point 16:** it advises the Saeima and the Cabinet on matters related to its tasks. The results feed policy advice.
- **Since 1 January 2022** Latvijas Banka is the national coordinator of the financial literacy strategy (transferred from the FCMC).
- **No new law or regulation is needed.** Latvijas Banka ran the first assessment in February–April 2025 (RAIT; 1,159 pupils; 75 schools).

**Schools' participation**

- Participation is **voluntary for schools**. The school head decides, as in 2025.
- There is no legal duty to take part. A regular schedule and a short questionnaire keep the burden low.
- **[verify]** whether IZM or VIAA must be informed or must agree when surveys run in schools.

**Data protection (minors, aged about 15)**

| Issue | Rule | Recommended practice |
|---|---|---|
| Anonymous questionnaire | GDPR does not apply to truly anonymous data (Recital 26) | Keep it anonymous: no names and no linkable IDs; report results by group only |
| If any personal data is processed | GDPR Art. 6(1)(e) (public-interest task, based on the Law on Latvijas Banka); Art. 89 safeguards for research and statistics; Latvia's Personal Data Processing Law | Inform pupils and parents; minimise data; process under a contract with the survey firm (GDPR Art. 28) |
| Adding a teacher module | Same rules; teachers are adults | Anonymous by default |

**[verify]** with Latvijas Banka's data protection officer how the 2025 survey was set up, and copy that model.

**Making the commitment stick**

- **The 2028+ national financial literacy strategy** is a partnership document coordinated by Latvijas Banka, not a Cabinet act. Write in "assessment every 2 years" as a measure with an indicator.
- **The Education Development Guidelines 2028+** are a Cabinet-approved planning document, binding on public administration as a plan. Add the 9th-grade index as an indicator.
- **Latvijas Banka's annual work plan.**

## B2. Opting into the PISA 2029 financial literacy module

**The legal position**

- **Latvia already takes part in PISA** (reading, maths, science) in every cycle since 2000, and is an OECD member since 2016.
- **The financial literacy module is optional.** Latvia took it in **2012 (501 points) and 2018 (501)** and skipped it in 2022. PISA 2025 had no such module.
- **No law or Cabinet regulation governs the choice.** It is an **administrative and budget decision of IZM**, communicated through Latvia's representation on the OECD PISA Governing Board.
- **Implementation** runs through the national PISA centre under contract with IZM. **[verify]** that this is the University of Latvia's Institute for Educational Research.

**Cost and timing**

- PISA 2029 offers the module for the fifth time. **It needs at least 10 countries.** The OECD sets the option's cost once the number of participants is known.
- Latvia does not pay the €234,000 entry fee for new PISA participants, because it is already in PISA.
- **The cost must sit in IZM's budget programme.** The annual State Budget Law and IZM's budget requests are the legal vehicle. Ask in IZM's 2028 budget request.
- **[verify]** the decision deadline for options. The PISA 2029 field trial is expected in 2028, so decisions are likely needed in 2026–2027.

**Data protection**

- PISA samples schools and pupils.
- The national centre processes pupils' data under the GDPR, with parental information. The OECD receives pseudonymised data.
- The existing PISA arrangements already cover this. The module adds questions and test items, not a new legal problem.

## B3. Our yearly public scorecard

**The legal position:** no permission is needed. We publish analysis of **public information**.

**Rules to follow**

- **Quoting and reproducing official data and charts:** cite the source. Short quotations for criticism and review are allowed under Latvia's Copyright Law. **[verify the article and Latvijas Banka's re-use terms for its charts]**
- **No presentation of our own survey as official data.** Accuracy also matters for the NGO's credibility.
- **Personal data:** none needed. Use only aggregate public figures.

## B4. Proposal to LSM (optional)

**The law** (Law on Public Electronic Mass Media and Their Management, version in force to 30.04.2027):

| Article | What it says | What it means for us |
|---|---|---|
| **9(5)** | For editorial independence, **specific programmes are not in themselves a separate subject of the public-service order** | We **cannot** legally ask for "a weekly finance show" in the order |
| **10(1)** | SEPLP leads the drafting of the public-service order and sets performance indicators | SEPLP is the addressee, not LSM's newsroom |
| **10(3)** | "When drafting the public-service order, **the opinion of as many different groups of society as possible is heard**" | **Our legal hook:** we are such a group |
| **11(1)** | SEPLP approves the **annual plan for the next year by 31 December**; LSM's boards submit proposals | The **2027 plan is due by 31 December 2026**, so it is very late for 2027. Realistic target: **the 2028 plan** (drafted during 2027) |
| **11(2)–(3)** | The plan contains a general content description and **tasks set by SEPLP**, with goals, tasks and **performance indicators** | Ask for a **task and indicator** on economic and financial literacy content, for example reach among 15–24-year-olds |
| **11(5)** | SEPLP may require other information only "insofar as editorial independence is not affected" | Keep the ask at the level of goals, not programmes |

**Our evidence for SEPLP:** its own 2026 survey.

- LSM users rating its knowledge about business positively fell from **48% to 43%**.
- SEPLP itself recommended stronger economics content.

**Timing caveat:** the law's current version runs to 30.04.2027, and the 2027 order is being prepared "in line with law amendments" (LV portāls, 2026). **[verify]** the new procedure before writing the proposal.

## B5. Risks for Idea 4

| Risk | Answer |
|---|---|
| Latvijas Banka does not commit to a schedule | Put it in the 2028+ strategy as an indicator; offer the scorecard as the public use of the data |
| IZM lacks budget for PISA | Make PISA secondary; the Latvijas Banka survey alone covers the main need |
| Privacy concerns from schools or parents | Anonymous design; the 2025 model; clear information letters |
| LSM proposal seen as interference | Address SEPLP, not the newsroom; ask for goals and indicators only (Art. 9(5), 11(5)) |
