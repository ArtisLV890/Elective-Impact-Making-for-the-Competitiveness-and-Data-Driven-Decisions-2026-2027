# Part B. Main slides

## Slide 1. Title
- **Say:** the paradox: compulsory, yet 36% never learn about money at school. Or the Telegram money-mule story.

## Slide 2. Our case in one slide
- **Say:** the three messages: the problem is real, we know what works, the fix is doable now (four ideas, one rule, no new law).

## Slides 3–8. The scale of the problem
- **Slide 4:** point at LV on the far right: 11% high literacy, joint last.
- **Slide 5:** Latvia has the highest share with no emergency savings (26%).
- **Slide 6:** ages 18–19 score 8.0 of 20; all adults 12.2.
- **Slide 7:** the knowledge score fell 5.1 → 3.9; compound interest almost halved.
- **Slide 8:** 15-year-olds score 12.3/20, the adult level.

## Slide 9. School is where teenagers learn least
- **Point at:** the bottom row: 36% never learn about money at school.
- **Say:** "Not the teachers' fault: no guaranteed time, and the training rules don't require it."

## Slide 10. Teenagers are already targets
- **Point at:** 48% offered easy money; 18% asked to be a money mule.

## Slide 11. Why now: fraud
- **Say:** losses almost tripled in two years (€8.5m → €23.7m); purchase scams are the most common.

## Slide 12. Why now: the policy window
- **Say:** cycles end in 2027, new evidence (OECD, PISA), political will (Saeima subcommittee, course redesign), and money is set in 2027.

## Slide 14. What works
- **Say:** +0.15 to +0.33 SD on knowledge; a mandate on paper fails (Cole et al.); real courses work (Urban et al.).

## Slide 15. Peru
- **Say:**
  - lessons inside the existing course, workbooks, 20 hours of teacher training;
  - students +0.16, teachers +0.32, and +0.46 for teachers who taught;
  - debt down 15 points where pupils were taught twice;
  - US$4.8 per pupil.
- **Warning:** a third taught nothing, because it was voluntary.

## Slides 16–18. Backup evidence
- **16:** activity without results.
- **17:** other countries.
- **18:** scam education must be repeated.
- Skip these if short of time.

## Slide 20. Our strategy: four ideas
- **Say each in one line:**
  1. training that counts;
  2. required training (highlighted);
  3. teach within the standard;
  4. measure and show.
- **Close with:** "Ideas 1 and 3 need no rule change. Idea 2 is one new point in a Cabinet regulation."

## Slide 21. Idea 1: Latvijas Banka's seminars
- **Today:** Latvijas Banka is not a course provider under Reg. 569, so its sessions are "seminars", capped at 12 hours in 3 years.
- **After:** VIAA (bylaws: teacher support and materials) or a university delivers them, and all hours count. Four 12-hour modules, A–D.
- **If asked:** "It needs a cooperation agreement and a programme description, not a rule change."

## Slide 22. Idea 3: within the standard
- **Point at:** the table: credit (5.4), phishing (3.3), compound interest (maths 3.2.4–3.2.6).
- **Say:** "Legal permission isn't the problem. Guidance, lessons and test questions are, and VIAA can add all three."

## Slide 23. Who we work with
- **Groups:** policymakers, Latvijas Banka and teacher training, schools, media.
- **The media ask:** goals and indicators from SEPLP, not a programme. The media law forbids ordering specific programmes.

## Slide 24. Roadmap 2026–2031
- **Milestones:**
  - meetings and drafts now;
  - first module Sept 2027;
  - point 20.2 applies Sept 2028;
  - PISA 2029;
  - compliance by 2031.

## Slide 26. Expected results
- **Policy by 2028:** four results, listed on the slide.
- **Learning by 2030:** the index 12.3 → 13.2 (base) / 14.0 (stretch); "never learn at school" 36% → 20% / under 10%.

## Slide 27. Value for money
- **Say:** a set-up cost in the low hundreds of thousands of euros, against €23.7m a year in fraud losses. Peru spent US$4.8 per pupil.

## Slide 29. What changes in the rules
- **Say:**
  - only Reg. 569 changes;
  - the standards stay;
  - commitments go into the guidelines and the 2028+ strategy;
  - quick wins now: the approved course, and the PISA module.

## Slide 30. The draft rule (point 20.2)
- **Point at:**
  - the Latvian text;
  - the plain-words box: who, what, how, when;
  - the 36-hour bar: 6 hours child rights, 12 hours finance, 18 hours free choice.
- **Say:** "No extra hours: we steer part of the training teachers already do, as point 20.1 does for swimming."

## Slide 31. The OECD plan
- **Say:** the OECD (Nov 2026) calls school financial literacy "essential". Action 4 has no owner yet; we offer to fill it.

## Slide 32. How the change happens
- **Steps:**
  1. IZM drafts with an annotation.
  2. TAP consultation.
  3. Coordination (local governments, unions including LIZDA, employers; silence counts as agreement).
  4. Cabinet.
- **Budget:** no new hours; courses via VIAA; the PISA module needs a line in IZM's 2028 budget.

## Slide 33. Our asks
- **IZM:** adopt point 20.2 and opt into PISA 2029 finance.
- **VIAA:** run the course and add the finance thread.
- **Latvijas Banka:** survey every 2 years and a school pillar.
- **Last line:** "Make it real, before the scammers teach our teenagers first."
