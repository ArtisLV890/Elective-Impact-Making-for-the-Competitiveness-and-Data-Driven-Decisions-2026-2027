# Presentations

## 24.11.2026 and 25.11.2026: Presentations and discussion of impact cases

Each presentation is 12–15 minutes and focuses on:
1. What issue are you planning to solve, and why is it important?
2. How does the research or data support your intention? Include a brief description of the research or data.
3. What is your strategy? What steps will you take to solve the issue, including interaction with media, policy makers and industry where appropriate?
4. What result do you expect (e.g. a change to a law or regulation)?
