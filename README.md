# Impact Making for the Competitiveness and Data-Driven Decisions

Elective course, academic year 2026/2027 · 3 ECTS · Course director: **Arnis Sauka** (arnis.sauka@sseriga.edu)

> This course is about making an impact: how to work with media, policy makers and industry associations to share knowledge, foster data-driven decisions and increase competitiveness. Students turn a "research product / knowledge" into a real, applicable tool at the industry or policy level, including interaction with media.
